## Evidence rules

For every observation, record:

- Date and time (with timezone).
- Device state and power source.
- Exact command, tool, version, and arguments.
- Unedited output where practical.
- Whether the result is **observed**, **inferred**, or **unverified**.
- Any redactions applied before publication.

Keep private identifiers and secrets out of public notes. Never overwrite the original firmware dump; analyze a working copy and record hashes.
